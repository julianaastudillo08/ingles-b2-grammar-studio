    01

    Tiempos verbales — panorama B2+

    En B2 ya no basta con “saber” cada tiempo por separado: importa elegir el correcto según si la acción es completa, en curso, anterior a otra, o relevante para el presente.

    Mapa rápido

    TiempoUso principalEjemplo
    Present SimpleHábitos, hechos, horariosShe works remotely on Fridays.
    Present ContinuousAhora / temporal / planes acordadosI'm meeting the client at 3.
    Present Perfect SimpleExperiencia, resultado presente, “ever/never”I've finished the report.
    Present Perfect ContinuousDuración hasta ahora, acción recién terminadaI've been working since 8 a.m.
    Past SimpleAcción acabada en el pasado (tiempo definido)We signed the contract yesterday.
    Past ContinuousAcción en progreso en un momento pasadoThey were negotiating when I called.
    Past PerfectAnterior a otra acción pasadaShe had left before we arrived.
    Future PerfectCompletado antes de un punto futuroBy June, I'll have completed the course.

    Present Perfect vs Past Simple

    Si hay una fecha o momento pasado cerrado → Past Simple. Si el tiempo no está cerrado o importa el vínculo con ahora → Present Perfect.

    I saw her last week. / I've seen her this week. La semana pasada (cerrado) vs esta semana (abierto).

    Perfect Simple vs Continuous

    Simple: cuántas veces o si está hecho. Continuous: cuánto tiempo o evidencia visible del esfuerzo.

    I've read three articles. / I've been reading all morning.
    02

    Futuro y planes

    Cuatro formas compiten: will, going to, present continuous y present simple. La elección depende de espontaneidad, evidencia y si es un horario fijo.

    FormaCuándo usarla
    will + infinitiveDecisión espontánea, promesas, predicciones sin evidencia
    be going to + infinitivePlanes/intenciones; predicciones con evidencia
    Present ContinuousCitas y arreglos con otras personas
    Present SimpleHorarios oficiales (trenes, programas)
    will be + -ingAcción en progreso en un momento futuro
    will have + past participleCompletado antes de un momento futuro
    Look at those clouds — it's going to rain. / I'll help you with that. / The train leaves at 6:15.
    03

    Condicionales

    Del hecho general al arrepentimiento pasado: cada tipo cambia el tiempo del verbo en la oración if.

    Zero & First

    If + present, present / will

    Verdades generales y situaciones reales/probables.

    If you heat ice, it melts. / If we leave now, we'll catch the train.

    Second

    If + past simple, would + infinitive

    Hipotético presente/futuro (poco probable o irreal).

    If I had more time, I'd learn Japanese.

    Third

    If + past perfect, would have + pp

    Arrepentimiento: pasado que no ocurrió.

    If they had informed us earlier, we would have avoided the delay.

    Mixed

    Pasado en if → resultado en presente, o al revés según el sentido.

    If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.
    Alternativa B2+ Unless (= if not), provided/providing, as long as, in case (+ present para futuro).
    04

    Modales avanzados

    Obligación, deducción, crítica y habilidad en el pasado.

    ModalMatiz
    must / can'tDeducción presente: seguridad alta
    might / may / couldPosibilidad (could más tentativo)
    should / ought toConsejo o expectativa
    mustn'tProhibición fuerte
    don't have toNo es necesario (≠ mustn't)
    should have / ought to haveCrítica: debiste y no lo hiciste
    needn't have + ppHiciste algo innecesario
    could have / might havePosibilidad en el pasado
    She must be stuck in traffic. / You needn't have bought so much food — we had plenty.
    05

    Voz pasiva y causativa

    Enfoque en el proceso o en quien recibe la acción; común en textos formales y académicos.

    Estructura

    be + past participle (+ by agent)

    Todos los tiempos: is made, was being discussed, has been approved, will be sent.

    Causativa (have / get)

    • have something done — pagas o encargas a alguien: I had my laptop repaired.
    • get something done — más informal, a veces implica esfuerzo: I finally got the form signed.

    Verbos de reporting en pasiva

    It is said/believed/reported that… / He is said to be…

    06

    Estilo indirecto (reported speech)

    Al reportar, los tiempos suelen retroceder (backshift) si el verbo introductorio está en pasado.

    DirectoIndirecto
    Present →Past
    Past →Past Perfect
    will →would
    can →could
    must (obligación) →had to
    "I'm leaving tomorrow," she said. → She said she was leaving the next day. Cambios de tiempo y lugar: today → that day, here → there.

    Verbos de reporting

    suggest + gerund / that-clause; deny + gerund; apologise for; warn someone (not) to; insist on / that.

    07

    Oraciones de relativo

    Definen o añaden información extra; en C1 aparecen formas reducidas y preposiciones al final.

    Defining

    Esenciales: who, which, that, whose, where. Sin comas.

    The candidate who speaks three languages was hired.

    Non-defining

    Extra info: comas; no se usa that; no se omite el pronombre.

    My manager, who lives in Bristol, works remotely.

    Reduced relatives

    The report submitted yesterday… (= which was submitted)

    Participio activo: people living abroad…

    08

    Wish, regret & preference

    wish + past simple

    Deseo sobre el presente (irreal).

    I wish I spoke fluent French.

    wish + past perfect

    Arrepentimiento pasado.

    I wish I hadn't missed the interview.

    would rather

    + infinitive (yo) / + past simple (otra persona).

    I'd rather you didn't share my number.

    It's (high) time

    + past simple → urgencia.

    It's high time we addressed this issue.
    09

    Inversión y énfasis (C1)

    Estilo formal y literario: el auxiliar va antes del sujeto tras ciertas expresiones negativas o restrictivas.

    • Never / Rarely / Seldom have I seen…
    • Not only did she apologise, but she also…
    • Hardly … when Hardly had we arrived when it started raining.
    • No sooner … than No sooner had I left than they called.
    • Only after/when Only when the data was verified did we publish.

    Cleft sentences

    It was John who fixed the bug. / What I need is more time.

    10

    Participios y gerundios

    Cláusulas participiales

    Having finished the task, she logged off. / Written in 2020, the paper remains relevant.

    Gerund vs infinitive (verbos clave)

    • Solo gerundio: enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, consider
    • Solo infinitivo: want, decide, hope, agree, refuse
    • Ambos, mismo sentido: begin, start, continue
    • Ambos, distinto sentido: stop, remember, forget, try
    I stopped to smoke. (paré con el fin de…) / I stopped smoking. (dejé el hábito)
    11

    Artículos y cuantificadores

    a / an / the / zero

    • Instituciones genéricas: go to university (sin the)
    • Único conocido: the internet, the government
    • Instrumentos: play the piano
    • Geografía: the Netherlands, Mount Everest (sin the)

    some / any / much / many

    Countable: many, few, a few, several. Uncountable: much, little, a little.

    few = casi ninguno (negativo); a few = algunos (positivo).

    12

    Conectores formales (escritura B2+)

    Elevan ensayos, informes y presentaciones: contrasta, causa, concesión y orden lógico.

    FunciónEjemplos
    Contrastehowever, nevertheless, whereas, while, on the other hand
    Causatherefore, consequently, owing to, due to (+ noun)
    Concesiónalthough, even though, despite / in spite of (+ noun/-ing)
    Añadirfurthermore, moreover, in addition
    Ordenfirstly, subsequently, ultimately
    Recuerda Despite/In spite of van seguidos de sustantivo o -ing, no de oración completa. Although va con oración.

    Phrasal verbs esenciales B2+

    Los verbos compuestos aparecen mucho en lectura y listening. Fíjate en si llevan objeto y si es separable (turn it off vs look after the kids).

    Trabajo y estudio

    carry out — realizar (un estudio)
    deal with — encargarse de / manejar
    figure out — averiguar
    point out — señalar
    set up — crear / montar

    Cambio y resultado

    turn out — resultar ser
    end up — acabar por
    give up — dejar / rendirse
    run out of — quedarse sin
    catch up with — ponerse al día

    No siempre coinciden con el español. Memoriza chunks: depend on, interested in, responsible for, good at.

    PatrónEjemplo

    noun + prepreason for, increase in, advantage of
    tiempoon Monday, in 2024, at night, by Friday (= antes del viernes)

    Formación de palabras

    Prefijos y sufijos para transformar palabras en examen: -tion, -ment, un-, dis-, -able, -ive.

    Sufijos frecuentes

    • employ → employment / employer / employee
    • decide → decision
    • able → ability / unable → inability
    • success → successful → successfully

    Prefijos

    • un- (unfair), dis- (disagree), mis- (misunderstand)
    • over- (overwork), under- (underestimate)
    • re- (rewrite), pre- (preview)
    16

    Concordancia y preguntas

    Sujeto-verbo, collective nouns, y question tags al final de frases para confirmar.

    • Each/Every/Neither + singular: Each student has a laptop.
    • News, advice, furniture → singular verb.
    • The police → plural; the government → singular (UK usage).
    • Question tags: You're ready, aren't you? / She left, didn't she?
    19

    Comparativos, superlativos y cuantificadores

    Más / el más; enough, too, so/such; much/many; fewer/less — errores frecuentes en ECAES.

    Formas

    • short adj: -er / the -est (big → bigger → the biggest)
    • long adj: more / the most (important)
    • irregular: good/well → better → the best; bad → worse → the worst
    • as…as · not as…as · the more…, the more…

    Trampas

    • less + uncountable · fewer + countable
    • enough + noun / adj + enough
    • too = exceso · very = grado
    20

    Preguntas e indirect questions

    Wh- questions, embedded questions (sin inversión), preposición al final en estilo formal.

    Do you know where she lives? (NO: where does she live)

    Could you tell me what time the meeting starts? · I wonder why he left.

    17

    Escritura B2–C1 (producción escrita completa)

    Ensayos, correos, informes y descripción de datos. Incluye plantillas, modelo comentado, banco de frases y taller interactivo con contador de palabras y rúbrica.

    Opinion / Agree–disagree

    To what extent do you agree? · In my view… · I partly agree because…

    Intro → 2 body (reason + example) → Conclusion

    Discussion (ventajas / desventajas)

    On the one hand… / On the other hand… · Overall, I believe…

    Problem–solution

    P1: describe problem + causes. P2–P3: solutions + who should act.

    Two-part question

    Responde ambas partes explícitamente. Un párrafo por pregunta + conclusión breve.

    Modelo B2 comentado (~230 palabras)

    In recent years, remote work has become increasingly common. While it offers clear benefits, I believe its drawbacks should not be underestimated.

    On the one hand, working from home can improve work–life balance. Employees save commuting time and may concentrate better in a quiet environment. For instance, a 2022 survey found that many professionals reported higher productivity when they controlled their schedules.

    On the other hand, remote arrangements may weaken team cohesion. Informal conversations and spontaneous collaboration are harder to replicate online. Furthermore, some workers struggle to separate personal and professional life, which can lead to burnout.

    In conclusion, remote work is neither entirely positive nor negative. Provided that companies invest in communication tools and clear boundaries, hybrid models are likely to offer the best outcome.

    Subrayado = conectores y marcadores útiles para copiar.

    Rúbrica B2 Task (respondes la pregunta) · Coherence (párrafos + linkers) · Vocabulary (menos repetición) · Grammar (variedad de tiempos, pocos errores graves).

    Formal

    • Dear Mr/Ms + surname / Dear Sir or Madam
    • I am writing to enquire / complain / apply…
    • I would be grateful if you could…
    • Yours sincerely (nombre conocido) · Yours faithfully (sin nombre)

    Informal

    • Hi / Dear + first name
    • Just wanted to let you know… / Thanks for…
    • Let me know if you can make it.
    • Best wishes / Take care / See you soon

    Plantilla formal (solicitud)

    Dear Professor [Name],

I am writing to request an extension for the [assignment] due on [date]. Unfortunately, [brief reason].

I would be grateful if you could allow me to submit the work by [new date]. I have already completed [progress].

Thank you for your consideration.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]

    Informe corto (survey / datos)

    1. Introduction — purpose of the report (This report summarises…)
    2. Findings — orden lógico (majority, minority, notable trend)
    3. Conclusion / Recommendation — one clear action

    Estrategias ECAES / Saber Pro

    Cómo abordar cada formato bajo presión de tiempo.

    1. Completar: lee toda la oración; identifica tiempo/marcador (since, by, yesterday).
    2. Relacionar: resuelve primero las parejas seguras; elimina opciones usadas.
    3. Unir: busca sujeto + verbo; cláusulas con if/when/although suelen ir al inicio.
    4. Corregir: revisa concordancia, preposición fija y uncountable nouns.
    5. Tiempo: ~1 min por ítem; no te atasques — marca y vuelve al final.

    Fichas rápidas de consulta

    Despliega cada ficha cuando necesites repasar en un vistazo antes del simulacro.

    Tiempos — señales temporales
    since/for → perfect · yesterday/ago → past · while/as → continuous · by + fecha futura → future perfect · already/yet/just → present perfect.
    Condicionales en una línea
    0: If + pres, pres · 1: If + pres, will · 2: If + past, would · 3: If + past perfect, would have + pp · Mixed: combina pasado con presente según el sentido.
    Modales — trampa ECAES
    mustn't ≠ don't have to · should have (no hiciste) · needn't have (hiciste de más) · can't = deducción negativa · must = deducción positiva.
    Conectores — estructura
    although + clause · despite/in spite of + noun/-ing · however/therefore + clause (coma) · owing to/due to + noun.
    Verb patterns
    enjoy/avoid/mind + -ing · want/hope/decide + to · suggest/recommend + -ing o that + base · look forward to + -ing · used to + inf (pasado) · be used to + -ing (acostumbrado).
    Falsos amigos top
    actually = de hecho · currently = actualmente · eventually = al final · sympathetic = comprensivo · sensible = sensato · assist = ayudar.
    Reading — señales de respuesta
    Sinónimos en pregunta y texto · negación (hardly, rarely) · pronombres (this/which = idea anterior) · tiempos verbales indican cuándo ocurrió la acción.
